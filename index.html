The storage wall is planned along the one uninterrupted wall in the living zone (no windows or doors), so it reads as architecture rather than furniture. Depth and exact run length should be confirmed once wall dimensions are verified.

Design logic

Living and dining stay one open volume, as in your existing plan — no new walls proposed. Seating turns to face the storage wall rather than the kitchen opening, so the lounge feels enclosed and calm even though the plan is open. The dining table sits on the through-route between entry and kitchen/stairs, so it doubles as a family activity table without blocking circulation.

Both existing walk-in closets are proposed as the "wardrobes" for each bedroom, refitted internally with a flush, handle-less door facing into the room — so no freestanding wardrobe competes with the calm wall lines you asked for.

Design logic

Bedroom 01 keeps the bed anchored to the one solid wall shared with the hall, facing the windows for morning light. Bedroom 02's storage bed sits opposite the door under natural light from its own window, with desk and bookshelf run along the corridor wall — so toys and books stay a step from the door, easy to tidy, out of the sightline when the door's open.
